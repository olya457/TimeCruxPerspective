1. Saying No to a Close Friend
Category: Boundaries Depth: 3/4 Estimated reflection time: 5–7 min
Situation:  A close friend asks to borrow a significant amount of money. You can technically afford it, but you do not feel comfortable giving it.
Block 1 — Single Choice
Label: WHAT IS MAKING THIS HARD?
Question:  What is the strongest reason you are hesitating to say no?
Options:
 I am afraid they will think I do not care about them.
 I feel guilty because I could afford to help.
 I worry this could damage our friendship.
 I do not know how to explain my refusal.
 I am not completely sure I want to refuse.
Block 2 — Write Your Own Answer
Label: NAME THE REAL CONCERN
Question:  If money were removed from the situation, what would you still be worried about?
Placeholder:  “Without the money part, I would still worry that…”
Max length: 300 characters
Block 3 — Multiple Written Answers
Label: SEPARATE CARE FROM OBLIGATION
Instruction:  Finish each sentence in your own words.
Field 1:  Helping my friend would feel right because…
Field 2:  Helping my friend would feel wrong because…
Field 3:  What I actually owe this person is…
Block 4 — Single Choice
Label: REVERSE THE SITUATION
Question:  If you were asking a friend for the same amount and they calmly said no, what would you consider reasonable?
Options:
 I would understand immediately.
 I would be disappointed, but I would respect it.
 I would probably take it personally.
 It would depend on how they explained it.
Block 5 — Final Conclusion
Label: YOUR CONCLUSION
Question:  After thinking through the friendship, your boundaries, and your responsibility, what do you want to do?
Placeholder:  “My decision is… The main reason is… I want to communicate it by…”
Max length: 500 characters
